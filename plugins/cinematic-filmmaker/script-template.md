# Script template

Fill this in with the user, top to bottom. Short is better: a first film of 30 to 90 s has 2 to 4 scenes.

## Logline
One sentence: who wants what, and what stands in the way.
> A night-shift waitress finds a letter a stranger left behind and must decide whether to chase him into the rain.

## Treatment
A paragraph per part: the start (who, where, what is normal), the turn (what changes), the end (what they choose).
Say the mood and the look in a line: "Quiet, lonely, warm light inside, blue rain outside."

## Cast
One line per character: @handle, age, look, voice in words.
- @june: 30s, tired eyes, mint uniform, hair tied back. Voice: low, warm, a little dry.
- @stranger: 60s, grey coat, hat, careful hands. Voice: soft, slow, slightly hoarse.

## Places and props
- @diner: a small 1970s diner at night, chrome counter, rain on the windows.
- @letter: a cream envelope, handwritten.

## Scenes
Number them. Each: where, when, who, what happens, how it ends.
1. INT. DINER, NIGHT. @june closes up alone. @stranger leaves money and an envelope, goes out into the rain.
2. INT. DINER, NIGHT. @june reads the letter.
3. EXT. STREET, NIGHT. She runs out; he is at the bus stop.

## Dialogue
In the format the Dialogue workflows read, so it goes straight in. One thought per line; an empty line is a longer
pause. Every @name must be an element with a saved voice.

```
@stranger (soft): Keep the change.
@june: We're closed, you know.

@june (whisper): "For the girl who always remembers the pie."
@june (tense): Wait!
```

## Before the shot list
- Is every line short enough to say in one shot (under about 10 s)?
- Does each scene end on a picture we can cut from?
- Is the whole film within the length the user wants? Add the shot lengths up.
