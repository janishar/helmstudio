# Sample plans

Three boards to start from. Change the beats to the brief; keep the order (style, characters, props, settings, beats,
frames, shots, sound). Lengths are at 24 fps. GPU minutes are rough guides for one Mac-class GPU: plan_board's
estimate for this install wins, and the user's limit (5 min by default) decides what asks.

## A 30 s ad

- Assets: 1 or 2 characters (Front and Side views), the product as a prop (Front), 1 place (Wide, Reverse).
- Lanes: Storyboard, Shots, Voice, Music, Sound.
- Beats: 6 to 8, 3 to 5 s each: the hook (0-3 s), the problem, the product, using it, the payoff, the end card with
  the name (hold it 3 s).
- Blocks: 7 frames, 7 shots, 1 to 3 voice-over lines, 1 music cue, 1 or 2 sounds.
- GPU budget: views 5 images; frames 7 Turbo drafts x 2 seeds, then 7 finals (a few minutes); shots 7 drafts, then 7
  finals (the bulk: 10 to 20 min, a card); sound a few minutes. About 20 to 30 min in all.
- Tip: the product's look must not drift: pick it in every frame it is in, and pin only takes where it reads.

## A 1 min story

- Assets: 2 or 3 characters (Front, Side, Back, one expression each), 1 or 2 props that matter, 2 places.
- Lanes: Storyboard, Shots, Voice (or a dialogue), Music, Sound.
- Beats: 12 to 16, 3 to 5 s each, in three parts: set up (who, where, what they want), the turn (what goes wrong),
  the end (how it lands). Open wide on the place; go closer for feelings.
- Blocks: 14 frames, 14 shots, 1 dialogue block per talking scene (each speaker's voice on the asset), 1 or 2 music
  cues (one per part, or one for the film), an ambience per place.
- Joins: cuts within a scene, a dissolve between scenes, a fade in at the start and out at the end.
- GPU budget: about 40 to 60 min; run it scene by scene (each its frames, then its shots), so each card stays small.

## A music video

- Start from the song: a music block first (or the user's track placed on the Music lane). Its sections set the
  beats: one beat per bar group, a cut on the downbeat.
- Assets: the artist (Front, Side, one expression), 1 or 2 places, a prop if it is the video's idea.
- Lanes: Storyboard, Shots, Music, Sound (often empty).
- Beats: 2 to 4 s each, faster in the chorus; e.g. a 2 min song is about 40 beats. Repeat the chorus's frames with a
  new angle rather than new places.
- Blocks: about 40 frames, 40 shots, 1 music block (every shot tied to the section it sits in).
- Joins: hard cuts on the beat; a morph or a dissolve only at a section change.
- GPU budget: the most of the three (1.5 to 2 h of shots): draft every frame on Turbo, final only the picked shots, and run
  section by section.
