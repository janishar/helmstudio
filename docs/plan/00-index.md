# Plan

How the frozen design gets built, and in what order.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `01-build-plan.md` | Ten phases with effort and a demo each, and why they are in this order |
| `02-milestones.md` | The eleven milestone breakdown and the review protocol |
| `03-delegation.md` | How work is split, verified and escalated when it is delegated |
| `04-origin-sketch.md` | The first sketch, before the design sprint — **superseded**, kept for history |

The operative briefs live in `docs/agents/milestones/`. This directory is the
reasoning behind them: what order, what risk, and what must not be compressed.
When the two disagree, the milestone brief wins — it is closer to the work.
