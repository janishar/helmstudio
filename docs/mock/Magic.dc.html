1 · Magic, its own page

Magic sits in the rail between Create and Gallery (owner: "make this separate feature than timeline no conflict or movement"). The board is lanes in time (owner: "imagine a lines in parallel, i drop at any point it opens a dialog"). Serial is blocks along a lane; parallel is blocks on different lanes at the same time. Lanes are free: any kind on any lane, named by the user. Every block is a node with a live preview (sketch, filmstrip, waveform, script line), its kind, length, @ picks, and status: Done, running with progress, Out of date (Shot 3, after Shot 2 changed), Draft. The pin marks a pinned take. The diamond is a Dissolve join, the bar a Cut, the edge shade a Fade in. "Grade · warm" is an effect badge. The link mark ties Line 2 to Shot 2 and the music to Shots 1–3. The player floats top right, small by default; it drags, resizes and shrinks to a bar. Shot 2 is selected; its details are in the side panel. Above the Storyboard, off the time ruler, is the Assets row: the board's own characters, props and places, with + Asset and Import (frame 8). Here the pointer is on @mara, so the blocks that use her light up and the rest dim.
