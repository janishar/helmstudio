How it works

plan 30 · docs/plan/30-cats-easter-egg.md

Trigger. Double-click the rail logo. A single click still goes home to Create. If you are already on Create the click does nothing, so a double-click never navigates twice; from another page the first click goes home and the double-click plays there.

Scenes. Four short scenes (7–8 s), one picked at random, never the same one twice in a row. One at a time: a double-click while a scene plays is ignored, then at most one scene per 10 s (the preview skips the 10 s rule).

Layer. A strip at the bottom of the content area, above everything, pointer-events: none: typing, clicking and scrolling go through it.

Reduced motion. With prefers-reduced-motion nothing moves: a still pair (he waves, she sits) fades in at the bottom right for 2 s and fades out. Try the toggle above the preview.

Themes. Fur is fixed; soft shading is a fur tone (no lines inside the figure); the outline is a token: on dark themes he gets a soft rim (--t2 at 50%), on parchment she gets a --dash outline. Her collar is --a2, his bell --a1.

Size. Inline SVG, CSS and a few lines of script, no files or network. Budget 24 KB raw, 8 KB gzip (the organic redraw measures about 20.5 KB raw, 6.2 KB gzip before minifying), loaded only on the first double-click. Phone: not shown (no rail logo there).
