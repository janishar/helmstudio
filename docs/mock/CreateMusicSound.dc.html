Create · Music and Sound effects

Two new kinds beside Image, Video and Voice (today's Audio, renamed so the three sound kinds read apart). Music: ACE-Step 1.5 (2B, the default), ACE-Step 1.5 XL, MiniMax Music 3. Sound: Stable Audio 3 Small-SFX (from words) and MMAudio large (matched to a video, or from words). Same page as the other kinds: the stage, the run steps, the take strip, the composer with its chips (each opens a small popup), the settings drawer open by default. All local models; times and memory are measured on a 64 GB M5 Pro. Every take kind here has its right-click menu (3d, 4b, 5c, 5d, 6).

Repaint on the strip (or in the menu) turns the stage waveform into a range picker: drag across it, or click a section above it to take that section. Only the range is made again; the rest stays. The new take goes into the strip beside the old.
