Timeline tools and zoom

Plan 23 (§27). Five options for the tool bar and the zoom control on the Timeline panel of Timeline.dc.html, each with Blade active, its cursor and cut line at 0:10.12, the playhead at 0:05.12 and the zoom at a mid level (the 21 s sequence and a little more in view; D: 9 s). Then the states every option shares. Tools: Select (A), Trim (T: edge ripple, cut roll, body slip, ⌥ body slide), Blade (B), Range (R), Overwrite (P).
