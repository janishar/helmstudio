Your machines

Runs where your GPUs are.

Apple Silicon

MLX or PyTorch MPS, per model. Weights load straight into GPU memory.

NVIDIA, one server or many

Add CUDA servers. Each job goes to one with the model and the room. In v1, CUDA runs Qwen Image; LTX video runs on Apple Silicon.

Private per person

Your gallery, pipelines and memory are yours alone. Accounts come from the admin.

# packs/qwen_image/model.yaml
id: qwen-image-2.1
family: qwen-image
runtimes:
  torch: {env: torch, devices: [mps, cuda]}
variants:
  base:  {dir: Qwen-Image-2.1}
  turbo: {dir: Qwen-Image-2.1-Turbo, shares: [text_encoder, vae]}
components:
  transformer: {precision: [bf16, int8]}
lora: {component: transformer}
Models

A model is one pack.

A YAML file and a small module per runtime. Its nodes and studio controls follow. Stack LoRAs and change their strength without reloading.
