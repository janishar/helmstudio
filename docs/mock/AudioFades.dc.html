0 to 10 s each. Drag a top corner of the clip, or type here.
  1. Every audio clip and every voice, music and sound block has a fade in and a fade out, 0 to 10 s (decision 1, "Per clip").
  2. The handles sit at the clip's top corners: hollow on hover, filled when the clip is selected, at the fade's end once it has one. Drag inward to lengthen; the fade snaps to 0.1 s, to the playhead and to other clips' edges.
  3. The curve is drawn over the waveform with the part it cuts shaded; the waveform itself shrinks under it, so you see what you hear.
  4. While dragging a yellow readout follows the handle: Fade in 1.5 s, Fade out 2 s. One undo step per drag.
  5. Timeline inspector: two new cells beside Gain, Fade in and Fade out, typed in seconds. Magic's block dialog gets the same two fields under Level.
  6. Send to Timeline carries each block's fades onto its clips; a sound lane's own fade in and out become its first and last block's fades.

2Crossfade between two music blocks

  1. Crossfade into next (right-click, frame 4) joins a music or sound block to the one after it on its lane. The default is 2 s.
  2. Longer cues (decision 2, "Make cues longer"): the outgoing block is made that much longer, so its take runs on past the cut; the striped tail is that extra, drawn over the next block's head. Both curves cross there.
  3. The badge is the join's transition chip, the look of the Shots lane's dissolve badges, in sky (audio) with the bowtie and the length: 2 s. Click it to set the length (1 s, 2 s, 4 s or typed, up to 10 s) or Remove.
  4. An older take without the extra tail plays as a dip: each side fades over half the length (1 s out, 1 s in). The badge turns amber with the warning glyph, and the warning offers Run again (makes the longer take) or Keep dip. The board's FixList counts it too.
  5. Changing the length on a block already made marks it out of date (amber ring) only when the new length needs more tail than its take has.

3Ducking music under voice

  1. One setting per board and per sequence (decision 3, "Auto, on in Magic"): Duck music, a switch and the amount, −12 dB by default (click it: −6, −9, −12, −18 dB or typed). On for a new Magic board, off for a new sequence. A board's setting goes with Send to Timeline onto the sequence it makes.
  2. Where it shows: Magic's Board panel (nothing selected), under Ask before runs; the Timeline's sequence panel (no clip selected), under the keys line.
  3. A ducked stretch is shaded sky on the music lane wherever a voice clip plays, with its amount on a tag; the waveform dips there and eases back over 0.3 s each side. It is worked out from the voice clips' times, so the player and Export match. Nothing to drag: move a voice line and the stretch follows.
  4. Music is a music block in Magic (or a clip on a track holding music on the Timeline); voice is a voice or dialogue block (or a voice clip). Sound effects are not ducked.

4Right-click menus

  1. One new group in the shared menu, after the level rows: Fade in…, Fade out…, Crossfade into next (music and sound blocks), Remove fades. Delete (Remove clip on the Timeline) stays last, in coral, as every menu has it.
  2. Fade in… and Fade out… open a small popover beside the menu: the length in seconds (the current one, or 1 s for a new fade), quick picks 0.5 / 1 / 2 / 4 s; Enter sets it. The row shows the current length on the right once there is one.
  3. Crossfade into next adds the 2 s crossfade (frame 2); greyed with No block after at a lane's last block, and it reads Crossfade 2 s… (opens the badge's popover) once there is one. Voice blocks do not have it.
  4. Remove fades clears both fades (and the block's crossfade); greyed with No fades when there are none.
  5. The same rows reach the Assistant (tool args, the director skill) and helm mcp, as every feature does.
