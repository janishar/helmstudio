1 · The Magic page

Magic in the rail between Create and Gallery. Top: the board name (switch or make a board), its size, Snapshots, Sends, Undo, Redo, Send to Timeline. Tools: Snap, Insert or Overwrite, + Lane, the side panel toggle (as on Create and the Timeline), Run board with its GPU-time estimate, and the zoom group on the right (out, slider, in, the span in view, Fit; ⌘ + wheel or pinch zooms at the pointer). The Assets row sits off the ruler. Lanes: Storyboard (sketches), Shots (filmstrips), Voice (amber orb, the line), Music (the album cover), Sound (sky orb), and an empty lane. Joins show their transition as an icon chip: Fade in at the start (a half bowtie), Dissolve 0.5 s (a bowtie), Cut (a line). Status on each block: Done, running %, Out of date (Shot 3, after Shot 2 changed), Draft. The pin marks a pinned take, the blue mark a tie, the pink pill an effect. Shot 2 is selected: its details are in the side panel. The player floats, small; drag, resize or shrink it to a bar.

2 · Add: drop at a point

Click or drop on a lane at a time. From Gallery places a take you already have there, at its own length (the lane's right-click has it too). Step 1: the kind, the length, Insert or Overwrite, and where it starts from. Step 2: the standard request for that kind, the same few fields for every model, the model's own settings under Advanced. A field the picked model can't do is greyed with the reason (for example MiniMax Music 3 has no key or BPM; MiniMax H3 takes both frames, 2c). Each form shows its GPU-time estimate before Generate. Before Create's Music and Sound ships, those two kinds are greyed (2b).

9 · Send to Timeline

Always makes a new sequence from the board as it is now. Shots go on V1 with their picked takes, lines and music on audio tracks linked to their shots. Cut and Dissolve go as they are; Fade and Wipe are baked into a short clip, a Morph is its own clip, so the Timeline doesn't change. A block with no take is a gap and a warning. The sends list keeps each one with its revision.
