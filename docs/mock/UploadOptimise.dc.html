Uploads · optimised in the browser

Every image and audio upload is optimised in the browser before it uploads: images to the largest size a model uses, WebP (else JPEG); wav to m4a, except a voice clip for cloning, which stays WAV. Video uploads are not now: frames and rows tagged "Not now · backlog" stay for the record and are not built. Only the pending tile changes; a finished upload is an ordinary take. LoRA uploads are not media and stay as they are. The tag is the item in 20-h3-options-and-uploads.md 24.9.
