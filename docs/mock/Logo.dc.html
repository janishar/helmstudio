helmstudio · logo concepts

Four marks for a local-first AI media studio (image, video, audio, voices) that runs on your own Mac and NVIDIA machines, where you steer the models like a helmsman or director. Each is drawn on a 24 grid in the theme accents, checked at 64, 32 and 16 px and in one colour. Plan: docs/brand/logo-plan.md.

1

Aperture helm

concept 1 of 4

Why. A ship’s wheel whose hub is a camera iris: you steer the models, the models make pictures. It grows out of the helm wheel the app already uses on Landing and Sign in, so nothing jars.

Risks. Wheels and apertures are common marks; six handles crowd at 16 px, so the favicon drops the blade lines and opens the hub. Reads as “settings” if drawn too thin.

Header size: helmstudio

2

Playhead h

concept 2 of 4

Why. An h whose stem is the Timeline playhead: a monogram for the name and for cutting takes into a sequence. It is the most “studio” of the four and owns the letter.

Risks. Says video and timeline more than image, voice or agents. The playhead flag looks like a bookmark or a pin when seen without the arch.

Header size: helmstudio

3

Spark tile

concept 3 of 4

Why. The Create spark inside the organic dock tile (three round corners, one tight, as in the dock and Sign in). It sits in the dock as one of the family and is the boldest at small sizes.

Risks. Four-point sparks are the generic “AI” sign (many assistants use one); low uniqueness. The tile is part of the mark, so it never sits flat on a photo.

Header size: helmstudio

4

Lens o

concept 4 of 4

Why. Wordmark first: the o of “studio” becomes a lens with a record dot, so the name itself is the logo. The lone o is the app icon and favicon.

Risks. A ring and dot alone is generic (record buttons, cameras, many apps); weak without the wordmark. The heavier o must be tuned by hand to sit on the baseline.

Header size: helmstudi

Recommend 1 · Aperture helm

It is the only one that says both halves of the name: helm (you steer the models, the judge and director agents) and studio (the camera iris for image and video). It keeps the wheel already on Landing and Sign in, holds at 16 px with the simplified hub, and has a natural loading state.

Pair it with the plain wordmark “helmstudio” (lowercase, Bricolage Grotesque 700, −0.02em) at 0.4× the cap gap. Keep the lens o (concept 4) as a fallback for places that need a type-only logo.

Open for the owner: pick 1–4, or 1 with the lens o in the wordmark; lime and orange split, or one accent.
