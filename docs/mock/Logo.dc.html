helmstudio · logo concepts · round 2

Round 2, after the owner’s note “more organic logo”. Four marks for a local-first AI media studio (image, video, audio, voices) where you steer the models like a helmsman or director: soft, uneven, living curves from nature instead of rings and spokes, drawn as clean vector on a 24 grid, checked at 64, 32 and 16 px and in one colour. Round 1 is kept at the bottom. Plan: docs/brand/logo-plan.md.

1

Leaf play

round 2 · concept 1 of 4

Why. A leaf whose heart opens into a play shape, on a stem that curls like a tiller: what you make grows from a seed of an idea, and you hold the stem. Soft, asymmetric, hand-cut.

Risks. Leaf marks are common in eco and wellness brands; the diagonal leaf fills less of the square, so it is the smallest at 16 px (the small cut widens the play cut). The play shape says video more than voice.

2

Sprout h

round 2 · concept 2 of 4

Why. A brush-stroke h, heavier at the foot and lighter at the top like a living stem, that sprouts a leaf flag: the flag is the vane you steer by, and the h owns the name.

Risks. A letter mark needs the wordmark nearby the first few times. The leaf must stay a clear flag, not a tick or an accent mark; in one colour it joins the stem and reads as a sprout.

Header size:

3

Bud pebble

round 2 · concept 3 of 4

Why. A river-pebble tile (soft, uneven, one tighter corner like the dock tiles) with a bud opening around a spark of light: the moment a take is born. Strongest at 16 px.

Risks. Cupped petals with a flame can read as a lotus, a spa or a candle; the pebble is part of the mark, so it never sits flat on a photo. Least about steering.

Header size:

4

Current eye

round 2 · concept 4 of 4

Why. A wave that thickens at the crest and curls into a helm turn, with an eye at its heart: you ride and steer the current of the models, and the eye is the lens and the director's look.

Risks. Spirals and wave curls are common; the thin tail fades at 16 px (the small cut is heavier). Must be drawn so it never reads as a 6, a 9 or an @.

Recommend 2 · Sprout h

It is the most organic and still the clearest: a living, brush-weighted h for helm, and a leaf flag that is both growth (what the studio makes) and the vane you steer by. It holds at 16 px, works in one colour, and the leaf gives a gentle loading state.

Pair it with the soft wordmark (Bricolage Grotesque 500 with a round-join stroke, so it reads like 600 with rounded corners). Keep 4 · Current eye as the runner-up if the owner wants a symbol rather than a letter.

Open for the owner: pick 1–4; soft or plain wordmark; leaf in orange or both parts in lime.
