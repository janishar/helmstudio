Leaf play

round 2 · concept 1 of 4

Why. A leaf whose heart opens into a play shape, on a stem that tapers and curls like a tiller: what you make grows from a seed of an idea, and you hold the stem. Review: the play cut now has bowed sides (no straight triangle) and the stem is one tapered filled shape, not a monoline stroke.

Risks. Leaf marks are common in eco and wellness brands; the diagonal leaf fills less of the square, so it is the smallest at 16 px (the small cut widens the play cut). The play shape says video more than voice.

Sprout h

round 2 · concept 2 of 4

Why. A brush-stroke h, heavier at the foot and lighter at the top like a living stem, that sprouts a leaf flag: the flag is the vane you steer by, and the h owns the name.

Risks. A letter mark needs the wordmark nearby the first few times. Review: the leaf used to float above the stem and read as an accent (ĥ); it now grows out of the stem tip, so in one colour it is one sprout. Keep the leaf wide enough that it never becomes a tick.

Bud pebble

round 2 · concept 3 of 4

Why. A river-pebble tile (soft, uneven, one tighter corner like the dock tiles) with two uneven petals cupping a seed of light: the moment a take is born. Strongest at 16 px.

Risks. Review: the round-2 flame in a cup read as a candle or lotus, and a first redraw as a figure with raised arms; now a closed pod around a seed. It can still read as an eye or a map pin. The pebble is part of the mark; least about steering.

Current eye

round 2 · concept 4 of 4

Why. A wave that thickens at the crest and curls into a helm turn, with an eye at its heart: you ride and steer the current of the models, and the eye is the lens and the director's look.

Risks. Spirals and wave curls are common. Review: redrawn as one clean curl (was about 60 traced segments with bumps), tail ends in a soft round terminal that holds at 16 px, eye moved to the optical centre. Must be drawn so it never reads as a 6, a 9 or an @.
