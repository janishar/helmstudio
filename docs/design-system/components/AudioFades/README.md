# AudioFades

Audio fades, crossfades and ducking (alpha.6, plan 41, API 1.106; board `docs/mock/AudioFades.dc.html`, owner 2026-11-28: "Per clip", "Make cues longer", "Auto, on in Magic", "Timeline too", "Keep these", "Sky", "Move them down"). Shared by the Timeline and Magic (`web_service/components/fades.tsx`, rules in `app/globals.css`).

- **Fade curve** (`fov`): an SVG over the clip or block (`inset 0`, `viewBox 0 0 w 60`, stretched), the part a fade cuts shaded `rgba(0,0,0,.42)` and a 1.6px non-scaling line from silence at the clip's edge to full at the fade's end (`t1`; amber `#f2c14e` while a crossfade dips). The waveform stays under it.
- **Fade handles** (`fh-h`): 12×12, 4px corners, at the clip's top corners on the waveform, under the title row (Timeline `top: 17px`, Magic `top: 27px`), each at its fade's end. Hidden at rest; hollow on hover (`s1` with a 2px `t1` inset ring); filled `t1` when the clip is selected (`fsel`); yellow `#ffe07a` with a 5px glow while dragged (`drag`). Ew-resize cursor. Drag inward to lengthen: 0.1 s steps, snapping to the playhead and other edges, never past the other fade or 10 s; one undo step per drag. On the Timeline they sit beside the clip's button (`fhs`, no control inside a button), above the playhead.
- **Drag readout** (`fh-rd`): a yellow `#ffe07a` tag over the handle, `#2a2000` 600 11px text, "Fade in **1.5 s**".
- **Crossfade badge**: the join's chip (TransitionMarker's look; on the Timeline `tlx`): the bowtie and the length, sky `#86cdea` ring (`xf`); amber `#f2c14e` ring and text with the lucide `triangle-alert` for a dip (`dip`). Above the playhead (z 9 in Magic, 21 on the Timeline). A click opens its popover (`xpop`, the ContextMenu's seconds look, 220px): "Crossfade", the length typed, picks 1 s / 2 s / 4 s, "Up to 10 s. Soft rain theme runs 2 s longer.", Remove in coral.
- **Blend tail** (`tl-ext`): the outgoing sound's extra past the cut, drawn over the next clip's head: 135° stripes of the lane colour at 26% (`--xk`: music violet `#d59bf6`, else sky) with a 1.5px ring at 70%, its own fade-out curve inside.
- **Dip warning** (`dipw`): an amber row (`#e8c468` 18% on `s2`, 1px ring, 12px corners): the glyph, the line, then its actions: Magic "Soft rain theme was made before the crossfade: it dips instead." with Run again (chip) and Keep dip (ghost); Timeline "… has no sound past its cut: it dips instead. Trim it 2 s shorter for a blend." with Remove (in the clip inspector).
- **Ducked stretch** (`duck`): over a music clip or block wherever a voice plays, a sky wash (`#7cc7f2` 20% fading down) between dashed sky sides, its amount on a tag at the top (`#7cc7f2`, `#06202e` mono 9px): "−12 dB".
- **Duck music row** (`srow`): a 34×20 switch (`dk-tog`, Toggle's look: `s5` off, `a1` on), "Duck music" over "Under voice · eases 0.3 s" (11px `t2`), and the amount (`dbc`, 28px mono on `s4`; `t2` while off) opening −6 / −9 / −12 / −18 dB or Typed… (−24 to −3). In Magic's Board panel under Ask before runs (on for a new board); on the Timeline's sequence panel under the keys line (off for a new sequence).
- **Playhead grabbed by its line** (`phg`, AudioFades frame 5): a 10px band (4px each side of the 2px line) along its whole height, ew-resize, a 9% `t1` wash on hover; held, the line glows (`grab`) and a yellow mono readout (`phr`, "0:10.00") follows the pointer. Snaps as the ruler does (Timeline: cuts and markers, N toggles, ⌘ inverts; Magic: frames). Nothing is stored. Fade handles and badges sit above it and keep their own drag.

Every one of these has its right-click rows (ContextMenu: Fade in…, Fade out…, Crossfade into next, Remove fades).
